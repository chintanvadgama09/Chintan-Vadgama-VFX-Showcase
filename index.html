Beyond the Tools

I am a multidisciplinary game developer with over 8 years of experience bridging the gap between engineering and art. From my early days modding GTA to leading art teams and optimizing entire production pipelines, my greatest strength is my versatility. If a tool doesn't exist, I build it. If a pipeline is slow, I automate it. If a team has a gap, I fill it.

What I Do Best:

  • Technical Art & Shaders: Master of NPR/toon shading and bridging 2D/3D workflows. Creator of custom shaders and tools across Unity, Blender, and Photoshop that reduce manual work to zero.
  • Art Direction & Generative AI: Proven track record directing titles like Epic Merge and Cooking Valley. Pioneer in adopting and training custom FLUX and Stable Diffusion models to supercharge studio pipelines.
  • Realtime VFX & Animation: Expert in Spine, Unity Particle Systems, and creating highly optimized hero effects for resource-heavy games like Call of Legends: RPG TD Game.
  • Optimization & Product Leadership: Experienced Product Owner capable of architecting 3D games (like Chef Factory) that run buttery-smooth on 2GB low-end devices without sacrificing visual fidelity.

Why Work With Me?

I am the ultimate "Swiss Army Knife" of game development. I speak the language of programmers, artists, and producers fluently, allowing me to foresee bottlenecks, lead teams with empathy, and deliver highly polished products on time.
